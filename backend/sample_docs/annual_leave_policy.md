# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Every employee accrues 20 days of paid annual leave per year, available from the start of the leave year.

## Requesting Annual Leave

Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Annual leave is subject to manager approval and must not conflict with team coverage or planned project deadlines.

## Carryover

Unused annual leave may be carried over up to 5 days into the next leave year. Any days beyond 5 are forfeited at the end of the leave year.

## Termination

Accrued but unused annual leave is paid out upon termination of employment.

## Approval Process

Leave requests are reviewed by the reporting manager, who may approve, reject, or propose alternative dates. Employees are notified of the decision through the portal.
