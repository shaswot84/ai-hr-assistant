# Casual Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Every employee receives 7 days of paid casual leave per year for short-notice personal matters that are not planned holidays.

## Consecutive Days

Casual leave may be taken for up to 3 consecutive days. Longer periods must be taken as annual or unpaid leave.

## Notice

Casual leave is requested with minimal notice through the HR portal and is subject to manager approval based on team coverage.

## Unused Casual Leave

Unused casual leave does not carry over into the next leave year and is not paid out upon termination.
