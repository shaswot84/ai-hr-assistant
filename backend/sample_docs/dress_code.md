# Dress Code

## General Guidance

Business casual is the standard dress code across all offices. Smart jeans, collared shirts, blouses, and closed-toe shoes are appropriate.

## Client-Facing Days

When meeting clients or attending external events, dress one level up from business casual — formal trousers or skirts, suits, or dresses.

## Fridays

Fridays are casual days: t-shirts and sneakers are acceptable, provided the attire is clean and professional.

## Exceptions

Roles with safety or hygiene requirements (e.g., warehouse, food service) follow the specific uniform or PPE rules for that role.
