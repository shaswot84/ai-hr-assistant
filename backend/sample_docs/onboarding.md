# Onboarding

## First Day

Your first day starts at 9:30 AM at the office reception. Bring a government-issued photo ID and your bank account details for payroll setup.

## Equipment

Laptops and accessories are issued by IT on the first day. Software accounts (email, Slack, HR portal) are provisioned before you join.

## Probation

New employees complete a probation period of 3 months. During probation, performance is reviewed at the end of each month.

## Training

Complete the mandatory onboarding training (safety, data protection, and code of conduct) within your first two weeks.

## Buddy Program

Every new hire is paired with a buddy for the first month to help with questions about the team and company culture.
