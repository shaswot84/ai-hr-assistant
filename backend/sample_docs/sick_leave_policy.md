# Sick Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Every employee receives 10 days of paid sick leave per year for illness or injury.

## Consecutive Days

Sick leave may be taken for up to 5 consecutive days without a medical certificate. Any period longer than 5 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

## Reporting an Absence

Notify your manager and the HR team before the start of your working day. Follow up with a written explanation of the absence.

## Documentation

For absences beyond 5 consecutive days, provide a medical certificate to HR within 3 working days of returning to work.

## Unused Sick Leave

Unused sick leave does not carry over into the next leave year and is not paid out upon termination.
