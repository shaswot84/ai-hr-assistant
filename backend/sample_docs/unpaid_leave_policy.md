# Unpaid Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Unpaid leave is available beyond paid leave entitlements and does not accrue any paid days.

## Requesting Unpaid Leave

Submit an unpaid leave request through the HR portal with the reason for the leave. Unpaid leave always requires manager approval and is granted only when paid entitlements are exhausted or do not apply.

## Effect on Benefits

Continuous unpaid leave beyond 30 days may affect certain benefits. Check with HR before planning extended unpaid leave.

## Effect on Accrual

No leave days accrue during unpaid leave.
